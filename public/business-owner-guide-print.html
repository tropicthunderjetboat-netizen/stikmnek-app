Business Owner Guide

Sign up · List your deal · Scan tourist passes

StikmNek  ·  stikmnek.com  ·  Online help: stikmnek.com/business-guide

In simple terms

You sign up as a business, submit your deal, and wait for approval. Then tourists with a StikmNek pass show you a QR code on their phone — you open the scanner inside StikmNek (from your Business Hub), verify the pass, and confirm. Most text and price changes you make later are checked again before they go live; turning your listing off or on is immediate.

1. Create your business account

You need an account so StikmNek knows who owns your listing and so you can open your Business Hub.

  1. Tap or click Sign In at the top of the website.
  2. Choose Sign Up, then pick Business (not Tourist).
  3. Enter your name, email, and a password you can remember.
  4. Check your email and confirm your account if asked — otherwise you may not be able to sign in.
  5. Sign in again. As a business user you are taken to your Business Hub (you can also use My Business in the top menu anytime).

2. Business Hub — where everything lives

After you sign in, your Business Hub is the sidebar area titled “Business Hub”. The menu names below are the exact labels you will see.

  1. Overview — Summary of your listing, quick actions, and a way to open the QR scanner.
  2. My Submissions — See listings waiting for approval or returned for changes.
  3. New Listing — Form to submit another business or deal.
  4. Edit Listing — Change description, prices, contact, WhatsApp, etc. (only after that listing is approved).
  5. Analytics, Reviews, Photos, Emails — Tabs for those tasks.
  6. If you have more than one listing, use Your Businesses in the sidebar to switch between them.

3. Submit a listing and follow approval

New listings are checked by the StikmNek team before tourists see them. This keeps quality high for everyone.

  1. Open New Listing in the sidebar.
  2. Fill in name, category, description, discount wording, prices, location, phone, hours, and optional WhatsApp.
  3. If you are a tour or activity, you can add tiered pricing (e.g. adult / child) in the pricing section.
  4. Upload clear photos (good light, show what you sell).
  5. Submit for approval.
  6. Watch My Submissions for status (pending / approved / needs changes). When approved, your deal can appear on the public site.

4. QR Scanner — how to check a tourist’s pass

When someone wants your StikmNek discount, they show a QR code from their pass on their phone. You scan it inside StikmNek (Business Hub) — you do not need a separate scanner app.

  1. In the Business Hub, open Overview, or use the floating Scan button on the edge of the screen when you see it.
  2. Allow the browser to use the camera when asked — scanning needs camera access.
  3. Ask the guest to brighten their screen and hold the QR code steady.
  4. Point the camera at their QR code. The app will read the pass and show who they are and whether the pass is valid.
  5. If you have several listings under one business, pick the correct listing when the app asks — the discount must match where they are redeeming.
  6. Confirm the redemption when prompted. That records the visit and applies your deal rules (including tiered prices for tours/activities, if you use them).
  7. If scanning fails, try a brighter screen, wipe the camera lens, or use manual code entry if the scanner screen offers it.

5. After approval — listing on/off and daily use

Once a listing is approved, you control whether tourists still see it on the map and deals list.

  1. Use Overview for a snapshot: redemptions, revenue-style summaries, and shortcuts.
  2. Active / Inactive (under Edit Listing) hides or shows your listing to tourists immediately — no need to wait for admin.
  3. Changes to text, prices, and photos in Edit Listing (except that on/off switch) usually go through admin review before they appear publicly.

6. Edit Listing (what changes and what waits for review)

Keeping information accurate helps tourists trust your business.

  1. Open Edit Listing. Use the tabs: Basic Info, Pricing, Contact, Preview.
  2. Update description, tags, location, discount label, prices, phone, hours, WhatsApp, and tiered prices (tours/activities) as needed.
  3. Submit changes for review. You may see a pending edit notice until an admin approves.
  4. Use Discard all or Reset section if you change your mind before submitting.

7. Photos, reviews, emails, and analytics

These tabs help you look professional and stay informed.

  1. Photos — Add gallery images; follow any moderation rules shown in the hub.
  2. Reviews — Read feedback and reply politely where the app allows.
  3. Emails — See messages StikmNek sent you (e.g. listing live, reminders).
  4. Analytics — Trends such as redemptions over time (available for approved listings).

8. Need help?

If something is confusing or broken, email stikmnek@gmail.com with your business name and a screenshot if possible. We aim to reply within one business day.
