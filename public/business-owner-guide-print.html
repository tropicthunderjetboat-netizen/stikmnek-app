Business Owner Guide

Sign up · List your deal · Scan tourist passes

StikmNek  ·  www.stikmnek.com  ·  Online help: www.stikmnek.com/business-guide

In simple terms

You sign up as a business, submit your deal in VT, and wait for approval. Complete your Business Profile and optionally upload credentials for verified trust badges. When tourists visit, they show a QR code — you scan it in your Business Hub, see the amount to charge in VT, confirm the visit, and give the StikmNek discount. Turning your listing off or on is immediate; most other edits wait for a quick admin check.

1. Create your business account

You need an account so StikmNek knows who owns your listing and so you can open your Business Hub.

  1. Tap or click Sign In at the top of the website.
  2. Choose Sign Up, then pick Business (not Tourist).
  3. Enter your name, email, and a password you can remember.
  4. Check your email and confirm your account if asked — otherwise you may not be able to sign in.
  5. Sign in again. As a business user you are taken to your Business Hub (you can also use My Business in the top menu anytime).
  6. On your phone, tap Save on the “Add to Home Screen” banner when it appears — this opens the scanner faster next time.

2. Business Hub — where everything lives

After you sign in, your Business Hub is the sidebar area titled “Business Hub”. The menu names below are the exact labels you will see.

  1. Overview — Summary, redemptions, quick actions, and open the QR scanner.
  2. Business Profile — Your business identity: name, category, map pin, logo, and contact details (complete this after your first listing is approved).
  3. My credentials — Optional insurance, permits, association docs, and first-aid certificates (see section 5).
  4. My Submissions — Listings waiting for approval or returned for changes.
  5. Edit Listing — Change description, prices, contact, WhatsApp, etc. (after that listing is approved).
  6. Analytics, Reviews, Photos, Emails — Tabs for those tasks.
  7. New Listing — Submit another business or deal.
  8. If you have more than one live deal, use the listing picker at the top of Edit Listing, Analytics, Reviews, or Photos.

3. Submit a listing and follow approval

New listings are checked by the StikmNek team before tourists see them. All prices are in Vanuatu vatu (VT).

  1. Open New Listing in the sidebar.
  2. Fill in name, category, description, discount wording, location, phone, hours, and optional WhatsApp.
  3. Enter original price (normal walk-in) and StikmNek deal price in VT — the deal price must be lower than the original.
  4. Choose how long your discount runs (start date and listing duration).
  5. Tours and activities: use tiered pricing — Adults, Children, optional Infants, and optional Private charter rows. Each tier has a standard VT price and a lower StikmNek VT price.
  6. Upload clear photos (good light, show what you sell).
  7. Submit for approval.
  8. Watch My Submissions for status (pending / approved / needs changes). When approved, your deal can appear on the map and deals list.

4. Business Profile (after approval)

Once a listing is approved, complete your Business Profile so tourists trust you on the map.

  1. Open Business Profile in the sidebar.
  2. Set your business name, category, phone, email, and hours.
  3. Tap the map to drop your pin — tourists use this to find you.
  4. Upload your logo if you have one.
  5. Save. This profile applies to your whole business, not just one deal row.

5. My credentials (optional trust badges)

Credentials are optional but help tourists see you are professional. Verified items can improve your rank on the StikmNek leaderboard. Tourists see verified badges on your listing — not your private documents.

  1. Open My credentials in the sidebar (available after your business profile exists).
  2. Upload any that apply: Tourism permit, Public liability insurance, Association credentials, First aid certificate (guides/drivers — include completion date).
  3. Accepted formats: PDF or image (JPEG, PNG, WebP).
  4. Tap Save credentials. The StikmNek team reviews uploads and marks them verified.
  5. If you replace a document, it must be verified again.
  6. Markets and small shops without insurance can skip this section — it is not required to list.

6. QR Scanner — verify a pass and charge the right amount

When someone wants your StikmNek discount, they show a QR code from their pass. You scan inside StikmNek — no separate scanner app. The screen shows how much to charge in VT (the StikmNek deal total), with tourist savings shown smaller underneath.

  1. In the Business Hub, open Overview or tap the floating Scan button.
  2. Allow camera access when the browser asks.
  3. Ask the guest to brighten their screen and hold the QR steady.
  4. The app shows whether the pass is valid and how many people (ages 6+) the pass covers.
  5. If you have several listings, pick the correct deal when asked.
  6. Tours and activities: enter how many adults, children (6+), and infants (under 6, usually free) are on this visit.
  7. Shops and retail (per-item deals): enter the number of items in this purchase — separate from people on the pass.
  8. Check Amount to charge (VT) — that is what the tourist pays you. Tourist saves (VT) is shown smaller for reference.
  9. Tap Confirm redemption. The visit is recorded with the correct tiered or flat pricing.
  10. If scanning fails: brighter screen, wipe the lens, or use manual code entry on the scanner screen.

7. After approval — listing on/off and daily use

Once a listing is approved, you control whether tourists still see it on the map and deals list.

  1. Use Overview for a snapshot: redemptions, summaries, and shortcuts.
  2. Active / Inactive (under Edit Listing) hides or shows your listing to tourists immediately — no need to wait for admin.
  3. Changes to text, prices, photos, and tiered pricing in Edit Listing (except that on/off switch) usually go through admin review before they appear publicly.

8. Edit Listing (what changes and what waits for review)

Keeping information accurate helps tourists trust your business.

  1. Open Edit Listing. Use the tabs: Basic Info, Pricing, Contact, Preview.
  2. Update description, tags, location, discount label, flat or tiered VT prices, phone, hours, and WhatsApp as needed.
  3. Submit changes for review. You may see a pending edit notice until an admin approves.
  4. Use Discard all or Reset section if you change your mind before submitting.

9. Photos, reviews, emails, and analytics

These tabs help you look professional and stay informed.

  1. Photos — Add gallery images; follow any moderation rules shown in the hub.
  2. Reviews — Read tourist feedback and reply politely. Tourists can review within 30 days of a redemption at your business.
  3. Emails — See messages StikmNek sent you (e.g. listing live, reminders).
  4. Analytics — Redemptions and trends over time (for approved listings).

10. Need help?

If something is confusing or broken, email stikmnek@gmail.com with your business name and a screenshot if possible. We aim to reply within one business day.
