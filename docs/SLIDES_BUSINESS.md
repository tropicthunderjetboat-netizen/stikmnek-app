# Slides — How StikmNek works (for Local Business)

> **How to use this:** Upload or paste this whole file into Kimi, Gamma, Canva
> (Magic Design), Tome, Google Slides, or PowerPoint and say:
> *"Make a simple, friendly slide deck from this. One slide per section. Big
> text, few words, use the visual ideas as image prompts."*
>
> Each slide below has: a **title**, a few short **bullets**, a **[Visual]**
> idea, and a one-line **Note** for whoever presents it.
> Keep the words simple. This is for business owners who may be new to apps.

---

## Slide 1 — StikmNek: Get more customers
- Free to join.
- Tourists find you and come to you.
- [Visual] A smiling local shop/tour owner welcoming a group of tourists.
- Note: Welcome slide — the big promise is more customers, for free.

## Slide 2 — What is StikmNek?
- Tourists buy one **Pass**.
- They look for deals on their phone.
- Your deal brings them to your door.
- [Visual] A tourist on a phone, an arrow pointing to a local business.
- Note: We connect tourists who already want to spend with your business.

## Slide 3 — Why join?
- **Free** — no cost to list.
- **More customers** — tourists discover you.
- **No commission** — you keep your money.
- [Visual] Three big icons: FREE, more people, a crossed-out percent sign.
- Note: We do not take a cut of your sales. Listing is free.

## Slide 4 — Step 1: Sign up as a Business
- Open the website.
- Choose "Business".
- Make your account (free).
- [Visual] A simple sign-up screen with a "Sign up free" button.
- Note: Pick the business option when you sign up.

## Slide 5 — Step 2: Make your profile
- Add your business name.
- Add a photo or logo.
- Add your phone and where you are.
- [Visual] A simple step-by-step form with a photo box and a map pin.
- Note: We guide you one step at a time — no long forms.

## Slide 6 — Step 3: Add your deal
- Add a clear title and photo.
- Add your normal price.
- Add your discount for pass holders.
- [Visual] A "Create deal" card with a photo, a price, and a "% off" badge.
- Note: A good photo and a real discount get you noticed.

## Slide 7 — Step 4: We check it, then it goes live
- We review your listing.
- Usually within about a day.
- Then tourists can see it.
- [Visual] A green tick / "Approved — now live" badge.
- Note: A quick check keeps quality high, then you are live.

## Slide 8 — Step 5: Tourist visits and shows a QR code
- The tourist shows their QR code.
- You scan it in the Business Hub.
- You give the discount. Easy.
- [Visual] A business owner scanning a tourist's phone QR with a tablet.
- Note: Scanning the code confirms they are a real pass holder.

## Slide 9 — Build trust (optional)
- Upload permits or insurance.
- Verified businesses rank higher.
- Reply to customer reviews.
- [Visual] A "Verified" shield badge next to a business name.
- Note: Optional, but trust badges help you stand out.

## Slide 10 — List your business free today
- Sign up free.
- Add your deal.
- Get found by tourists.
- [Visual] Big QR code linking to **stikmnek.com/business/new**, tagline "List free. Get customers."
- Note: End with the sign-up link and a QR so they can start now.

---

### Tip for the designer / AI tool
- Use a warm, tropical colour palette (teal, sand, sunset orange).
- Big headings, very few words per slide, one image per slide.
- The app shows in English, French, and Bislama — mention this if useful.
- Keep it encouraging and simple — many owners are new to apps.
