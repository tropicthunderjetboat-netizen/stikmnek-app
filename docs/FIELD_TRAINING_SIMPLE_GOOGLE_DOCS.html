PART 1 — Your job (read this first)

You visit businesses in person with the StikmNek company phone.

Your main workflow:

  1. Get their interest — use the introduction in Part 2
  2. Set them up — you do almost everything on the company phone (Staff → Onboard)
  3. Give them their login — write email + password on a card or paper
  4. Teach them — sign in on their phone, open Business Hub, practice scanning
  5. Practice scan — use the printed StikmNek practice QR code you carry

You DO:

  • Use the Staff login on the company phone
  • Complete onboarding for the owner (account + listing + photos)
  • Check quality before approving (Staff → Approvals)
  • Teach the owner and at least one worker how to scan
  • Leave a printed owner guide if you have one

You DO NOT:

  • Say they must pay to list — listing is 100% free
  • Promise a fixed number of tourists tomorrow
  • Walk away after only talking — finish setup or book a return visit
  • Approve blurry photos, wrong prices, or fake details

One business is DONE when:

  1. Account + listing created by you on the company phone
  2. Listing is approved and live
  3. Owner signed in on their own phone
  4. Owner or staff completed a practice scan (printed QR is fine)

PART 2 — Getting their interest (strong introduction)

This is the most important part. If they are not interested, nothing else matters. Smile, stand tall, speak clearly.

Step 1 — Walk in with confidence

Say hello to whoever is at the counter. Ask for the owner or manager. If they are busy, say: “I only need five minutes — it’s free and it brings tourists to your business.”

Step 2 — Your 60-second pitch (learn this by heart)

“Hi, I’m [your name] from StikmNek. We’re building the official tourist discount pass for Vanuatu — like a digital membership card tourists buy before they arrive.

When tourists visit Port Vila, they open our website, see your business on the map and deals list, and come to you for your special price.

They show a QR code on their phone. You scan it — takes five seconds — and you charge them your StikmNek price in vatu.

You never pay us anything to join. You choose your own discount. We only earn when tourists buy the pass — not from you.

I can put you on the app today from my phone. It takes about 15 minutes. Would you like to be one of the first businesses in [their area] on the map?”

Step 3 — Three reasons they should say yes (pick what fits)

  • Free advertising — tourists see them before they land in Vanuatu
  • No risk — they choose the discount; they can turn the deal off any day
  • Early advantage — we are signing businesses now; early listings get more visibility
  • Simple — we set it up for them; scanning is like checking a ticket

Step 4 — Show them (if you have signal)

Open www.stikmnek.com on the company phone. Show:

  • The Deals page — real businesses with photos and prices
  • The Map — tourists use this to find places

Say: “This is where tourists look. We want your business here.”

Step 5 — Ask for the yes

Do not ask “Do you want to think about it?” first. Ask:

“Can we do your free listing now? I’ll need your business name, a few photos, and your normal price and discount price in vatu. I do the typing — you just answer questions.”

Printed materials:

  • StikmNek practice QR code (for scan training after approval)

Gather from the owner (ask out loud, write on paper):

  • Business / deal name

PART 4 — Set up the business (company phone — YOU do this)

Almost all setup happens on the StikmNek company phone. The owner watches and answers questions.

A. Sign in as staff

  1. Open Chrome or Safari → www.stikmnek.com
  2. Sign in with the StikmNek staff account
  3. Tap Staff in the top menu (Staff dashboard)
  4. Tap the Onboard tab

B. Step 1 — Create the owner login

  1. Enter owner name
  2. Enter email (their address — double-check spelling)
  3. Enter a password (simple but safe — e.g. two words + numbers)
  4. Tap Create login / continue

C. Step 2 — Business profile + first deal (listing form)

Fill in every field carefully. This is what tourists see.

  1. Deal title — clear name (e.g. “Sunset Dinner Special”)
  2. Category
  3. Description — short, honest, friendly
  4. Discount label — e.g. “20% OFF”
  5. Original price (VT) — normal walk-in price
  6. Deal price (VT) — StikmNek price (must be less)
  7. Discount dates — start date and how long the deal runs
  8. Location / address
  9. Phone and WhatsApp
  10. Hours
  11. Photos — upload 3–5 (bright, real, their business)
  12. Tours & activities: fill Adult / Child / Infant tiers if needed
  13. Submit the listing

Price example (write on paper for them)

D. After submit — finishing touches

  1. Upload logo if they have one → tap Save logo
  2. Copy email and password from the success screen
  3. Write them on a login card for the owner
  4. Tell them they can change password later via “Forgot password”

E. Approve the listing (if needed)

  1. Tap Approvals tab
  2. Find their submission — check photos, prices, phone, address (see Part 8 checklist)
  3. Tap Approve if everything is good

Photo rules: daylight, in focus, real business only — no stolen internet images.

PART 5 — Teach the owner (their phone — THEY learn this)

Setup is done. Now they must know what to do when a tourist walks in. Go slow. Do it twice.

5A. Sign in on their phone

  1. www.stikmnek.com → Sign In
  2. Email + password from the login card you gave them
  3. Tap My Business — this is their Business Hub
  4. Optional: tap Add to Home Screen when the phone offers it

5B. Show them the menu (point at each item)

Scan a Pass / Scan QROpens the scanner (bottom green button too)
My SubmissionsSee if listing is pending or approved
Edit ListingChange deal — Active/Inactive turns deal on/off immediately
Business ProfileLogo, map pin, contact (after approval)
AnalyticsHow many tourists scanned

5C. Practice scan with the PRINTED QR code

Important: Scanning only works after their listing is Approved. Use the printed practice QR StikmNek gave you — same steps as a real tourist.

  1. Owner opens My Business
  2. Tap Scan a Pass or Scan QR
  3. Tap Allow when asked for camera
  4. Point phone at the printed practice QR code
  5. Read the screen: valid pass? Amount in VT?
  6. Pick listing if asked
  7. Tap Confirm redemption

Teach every staff member who serves tourists:

  1. Tourist shows QR — screen bright
  2. Staff opens scanner
  3. Scan
  4. Read VT amount on screen
  5. Confirm — then give the discount on the bill

Leave the printed QR at the counter for extra practice. Invalid pass = no discount.

If scan fails

  • Camera blocked → phone Settings → allow Camera for the browser
  • QR won’t read → hold steady, clean lens, brighter screen on QR
  • “Not available yet” → listing not approved — check Approvals
  • Wrong account → they must use Business login, not Tourist

PART 8 — Approval checklist (before you tap Approve)

  • Real business — you visited them
  • Name matches shop / tour
  • Photos are theirs, clear, daylight
  • Prices in VT are realistic — deal lower than normal
  • Phone works (call it in front of them if unsure)
  • Address makes sense
  • Description not empty
  • Category correct

PART 9 — Rules (never break)

  1. Never say they must pay to list.
  2. Never promise how many tourists will come.
  3. Never approve junk listings to go faster.
  4. Always: tourist pays StikmNek; business gives their listed discount.

PART 10 — Before you leave the shop

  • Listing live on StikmNek (you checked on the website)
  • Login card in owner’s hand
  • Owner signed in on their phone once
  • Practice scan done (printed QR)
  • At least one other staff member watched
  • Your number saved in their phone
