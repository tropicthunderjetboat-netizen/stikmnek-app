# Business picture flow — for sharing on WhatsApp / Facebook / Instagram

A simple 6-step picture story that shows a Ni-Vanuatu business owner how StikmNek
works. Made for phones. Big pictures, few words, Bislama first.

**Use `docs/social-cards/mobile/`** (`business_step1.png` … `business_step6.png`).
These are clean **1080×1920 (9:16)** cards that fill a phone screen — perfect for
WhatsApp Status, Reels, Stories and Facebook. Each card has the picture on top,
a numbered badge, and a **teal panel** at the bottom with room for your caption.

> Post the caption below as the text with each picture, **or** type it onto the
> teal panel in the free Canva phone app. Bislama is the main line; English is the
> small line.
>
> The clean source illustrations (no number, no panel) are in
> `docs/social-cards/scenes/` if you ever want to redesign a card. Run
> `npm run cards:mobile` to rebuild the finished cards.

## How to post it

**Facebook / Instagram (carousel — best):**
1. New post → add photos → choose `business_step1.png` through `business_step6.png` **in order**.
2. In the post text, put a short intro + the captions, e.g.
   *"Listem bisnis blong yu long StikmNek — i fri! 👇"*
3. People swipe through the 6 steps.

**WhatsApp:**
- Use the **`mobile/`** cards. Send the 6 images **in order** in the chat or a group, each with its caption.
- Or post them one by one to your **Status** — the tall cards show full-screen.

**Instagram / Facebook Stories or Reels:**
- Use the **`mobile/`** cards. Post them as a sequence of Stories, or stitch them into a short Reel.

## Tips
- Always keep the **same order (1→6)** so the story makes sense.
- If you want the Bislama words **on** the picture, add them with the free
  **Canva** phone app or the text tool inside Instagram/WhatsApp — keep it big.
- End every post with the link: **stikmnek.com/business/new**
