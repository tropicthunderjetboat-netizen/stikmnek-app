# Slides — How StikmNek works (for Tourists)

> **How to use this:** Upload or paste this whole file into Kimi, Gamma, Canva
> (Magic Design), Tome, Google Slides, or PowerPoint and say:
> *"Make a simple, friendly slide deck from this. One slide per section. Big
> text, few words, use the visual ideas as image prompts."*
>
> Each slide below has: a **title**, a few short **bullets**, a **[Visual]**
> idea, and a one-line **Note** for whoever presents it.
> Keep the words simple. This is for first-time users.

---

## Slide 1 — StikmNek: Save money in Vanuatu
- One pass. Many discounts.
- Eat, tour, relax — and pay less.
- [Visual] A happy tourist at a Vanuatu beach holding a phone showing a QR code.
- Note: This is the welcome slide — say the name and the big promise.

## Slide 2 — What is StikmNek?
- A simple app on your phone.
- You buy one **Pass**.
- The Pass gives you discounts at many local places.
- [Visual] Phone in the middle, arrows out to a restaurant, a tour boat, a spa, a shop.
- Note: One pass unlocks lots of deals — that's the whole idea.

## Slide 3 — Step 1: Sign up (free)
- Open the app or website.
- Make an account in 1 minute.
- [Visual] A clean sign-up screen with a big "Sign Up" button.
- Note: Signing up is free and fast.

## Slide 4 — Step 2: Get your Pass
- Choose how many people.
- Choose your dates.
- Pay by card. Done.
- [Visual] A simple "Build your Pass" screen with a people counter and a calendar.
- Note: You tell it your group and dates, then pay — that's it.

## Slide 5 — How much does it cost?
- **A$15** for the first person.
- **A$10** for each extra person.
- Children **under 6 are free**.
- [Visual] Three icons: 1 adult = A$15, +1 adult = A$10, small child = FREE.
- Note: Simple, flat pricing — no surprises. Prices are in Australian dollars.

## Slide 6 — Two kinds of Pass
- **1-Day Pass** — great for a day trip or cruise stop.
- **7-Day Holiday Pass** (+A$15) — best for a holiday.
- Share the app with friends → get **7 extra days free** (14 days total).
- [Visual] Two cards side by side: "1 Day" and "7 Days" with a "+7 FREE" ribbon.
- Note: The 7-day pass is the popular one — and sharing doubles your days.

## Slide 7 — Step 3: Find deals
- Browse by type: Dining, Tours, Activities, Spa, Shopping, Stays.
- Or open the **map** to see deals near you.
- [Visual] A map of Vanuatu with coloured pins, and category icons along the top.
- Note: Find deals by category or just look at the map around you.

## Slide 8 — Step 4: Show your QR code and save
- Go to the business.
- Show your QR code on your phone.
- They scan it. You get your discount.
- [Visual] A waiter scanning a phone's QR code at a restaurant table.
- Note: No cash games, no paper — just show the code and save.

## Slide 9 — Leave a review (optional)
- Tell others about your visit.
- Give stars and a short comment.
- [Visual] Five gold stars with a smiling face.
- Note: Reviews help other tourists and help good businesses stand out.

## Slide 10 — Start saving today
- Sign up free.
- Build your Pass.
- Save all over Vanuatu.
- [Visual] Big QR code linking to **stikmnek.com**, with the tagline "Save money in Vanuatu".
- Note: End with the website and a QR so people can join on the spot.

- Use a warm, tropical colour palette (teal, sand, sunset orange).
- Big headings, very few words per slide, one image per slide.
