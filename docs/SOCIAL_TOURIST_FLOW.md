# Tourist picture flow — for sharing on WhatsApp / Facebook / Instagram

A simple 6-step picture story that shows a tourist how StikmNek works. Made for
phones. Big pictures, few words, English.

**Use `docs/social-cards/mobile/`** (`tourist_step1.png` … `tourist_step6.png`).
These are clean **1080×1920 (9:16)** cards that fill a phone screen — perfect for
WhatsApp Status, Reels, Stories and Facebook. Each card has the picture on top,
a numbered badge, and a **coral panel** at the bottom with room for your caption.

> Post the caption below as the text with each picture, **or** type it onto the
> coral panel in the free Canva phone app.
>
> The clean source illustrations (no number, no panel) are in
> `docs/social-cards/scenes/` if you ever want to redesign a card. Run
> `npm run cards:mobile` to rebuild the finished cards.

## How to post it

**Facebook / Instagram (carousel — best):**
1. New post → add photos → choose `tourist_step1.png` through `tourist_step6.png` **in order**.
2. In the post text, put a short intro + the captions, e.g.
   *"Save money on your Vanuatu holiday with StikmNek 👇 One pass, discounts everywhere."*
3. People swipe through the 6 steps.

**WhatsApp:**
- Use the **`mobile/`** cards. Send the 6 images **in order**, each with its caption, or post them to **Status** (full-screen).

**Instagram / Facebook Stories or Reels:**
- Use the **`mobile/`** cards. Post them as a sequence of Stories, or stitch them into a short Reel.

## Tips
- Keep the **same order (1→6)** so the story makes sense.
- End every post with the link: **stikmnek.com**
- Pricing is in Australian dollars (AUD).
